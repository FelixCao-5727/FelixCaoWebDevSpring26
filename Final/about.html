About NYU Swipe Marketplace

NYU Swipe Marketplace is a student‑run platform to adress the premier student-led solution to one of campus life’s most persistent headaches: the meal plan gap. Whether you’re a senior with a surplus of credits or a first-year running low on fuel during finals week, our platform is designed to keep the NYU community fed, frugal, and connected.

Empowering the Student Economy

    The primary advantage of our platform is mutual value. We recognize that the traditional dining model can be rigid; meal swipes often expire at the end of the term, representing lost money for students. By leveraging real-time data and an intuitive interface, we’ve taken the "swipe struggle" out of the equation. Welcome to a smarter way to dine at NYU. Beyond the financials, the marketplace fosters a sustainable campus ecosystem. We reduce food waste by ensuring credits are actually utilized and build a stronger sense of community by facilitating face-to-face interactions among students from Tandon to Washington Square.

Simple, Seamless, Secure

    Our platform is built for speed, fitting perfectly into a busy student schedule.

    List or Browse: Sellers click "Sell" to post their availability, specifying the dining hall (like Lipton or Weinstein), the quantity of swipes, and the price.

    Smart Filtering: Buyers use our marketplace grid to filter by price, location, or urgency. If you need a meal right now, our urgency tags help you find a seller already on-site.

    The Transaction: Once a buyer finds a suitable listing, the platform facilitates the connection. Students meet at the designated dining hall entrance to complete the swipe-in.
